Exam strategy

How to Revise Science

Use this simple cycle for every chapter.

1️⃣

Understand

Read the overview and concepts. Ask yourself what causes the process and what its result is.

2️⃣

Practise

Write equations, draw diagrams from memory and solve numerical questions where applicable.

3️⃣

Recall

Close the notes and explain the topic in your own words. Then check the revision list.
